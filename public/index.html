Have you had situations where a process breaks down because it's moving between systems?"

Competitive Battle Cards

Every competitor you will hit in NSW State and Local Government — where they are strong, where Salesforce wins, and the exact conversation to have. Click any card for AI-generated coaching.

Slack vs Microsoft Teams PITCH

The opportunity: Every council and state agency uses Teams. Slack + Agentforce is the $20K door-opener — start with one use case, one team, expand.

What Teams can't do: External collaboration with citizens/contractors, AI-native workflow automation, Agentforce integration for intelligent routing.
Your line:"You're on Teams — totally understand. We're not looking to replace it. One use case: your planning team coordinating on a complex DA. Slack Connect lets you pull in the applicant, the architect, and your internal team in one channel. With Agentforce, status updates become automatic. Would that save your team time?"

Microsoft VS

What they say: "We have a whole-of-government Microsoft agreement. Everything runs through Teams and Power Platform."

The truth: M365 is collaboration. Power Platform handles simple automation. Neither manages complex multi-party citizen journeys or grant programs at scale.
Your line:"The WofG Microsoft deal is great for internal collaboration — but when a citizen submits a grant application that touches 4 departments, Power Platform can't manage that workflow. That's where we sit. Have you had situations where a process breaks down moving between systems?"

Proof point: Queensland QBCC just ran an Agentforce PoC for licensing triage — replacing a Microsoft workflow. Salesforce delivered in weeks.

ServiceNow VS

What they say: "We use ServiceNow for our ITSM. It handles everything."

The truth: ServiceNow is built for IT service management. It struggles with citizen-facing case management, grant programs, and multi-party journeys. Different architecture.
Your line:"ServiceNow owns your ITSM — that makes sense. When you need to manage a citizen application that crosses multiple agencies, or a grant program with 500 applicants, how does ServiceNow handle that? We've seen councils find that's where the gap opens up."

Proof point: Queensland Family & Child Commission went live on Salesforce in 3 months for their Reportable Conduct Scheme. ServiceNow couldn't handle the case management complexity.

Technology One VS

What they say: "We're on TechOne. It's deeply embedded in our council."

The truth: TechOne owns financials and back-office. It doesn't own citizen engagement, case management, or AI-driven service delivery. The gap is the citizen front door.
Your line:"TechOne is solid on the back office — financials, procurement. When a resident calls about a DA status or wants to report a missed bin online, where does that sit? That's the gap we solve — the citizen front door."

Civica Authority / Ci Anywhere VS

What they say: "Civica handles our permits and licensing."

The truth: Civica has point solutions for specific council workflows. Salesforce provides a unified platform — permit, complaint, and grant all in one system with one citizen view.
Your line:"Civica does a good job on specific workflows. The challenge we hear is when you've got Civica for permits, something else for complaints, something else for grants — your team is re-entering data three times. Does that sound familiar?"

SAP VS

What they say: "SAP handles our finance and HR."

The truth: SAP is a back-office ERP — finance, HR, procurement in large state agencies. It doesn't touch citizen services, case management, or grant programs. MuleSoft connects them.
Your line:"SAP owns your finance and HR — that makes sense. The citizen-facing side — service requests, grants, case management — that's a different layer. We sit on top of SAP, not instead of it. MuleSoft pulls the data together."

Oracle VS

What they say: "We run Oracle for our financials."

The truth: Oracle is back-office ERP. Like SAP, it doesn't manage citizen engagement or case journeys. MuleSoft integrates cleanly with Oracle.
Your line:"Oracle is solid on the finance side. The gap we're talking about is the citizen engagement layer — the front door of your agency. MuleSoft pulls Oracle data into the unified view so nothing falls through."

Workato VS

What they say: "We use Workato for our integrations."

The truth: Workato is a workflow automation tool — it connects systems but doesn't manage the citizen relationship or end-to-end case journey.
Your line:"Workato is a great integration layer — it moves data between systems. The question is what system sits at the centre managing the relationship. Are you using Workato to connect things to something, or is it the destination?"
